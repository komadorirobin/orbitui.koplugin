# OrbitUI ornament catalog 1

The first separate ornament catalog for **OrbitUI 0.1.0-alpha.54 or newer**.
This is a data-only add-on release, not a plugin update or beta announcement.

Open **OrbitUI settings > Add-ons > Ornament add-ons > Check ornament updates**.
Choose a pack, preview/install it, then **Use in current theme**. New packs are
optional and disabled until enabled. Do not unpack these ZIPs over the plugin.

- Manga: the existing Manga, Manga II and Manga III pieces in one managed pack.
- Japan: Japanese-themed decorations.
- Ukiyo-e Gallery: framed museum prints.
- Modernists, Authors and Authors II: author ornaments with biographical cards.

All six packs start at version 1. Their 60 information cards include English and
Swedish, selected by KOReader's language. **Placement** defaults to matching books
and can be changed to random per pack. Existing edited captions and artwork take
precedence; old native artwork is not deleted during adoption.

Artwork is unchanged from the previous plugin distribution. Each ZIP includes
its own sources, credits and license notices; conditions vary and some material
is noncommercial. Unofficial thematic/fan artwork is not endorsed by the original
creators. OrbitUI's code license does not relicense this artwork.

The catalog and ZIP hashes are verified by the add-on manager. Keep a backup and
test installation, cancellation, placement, restart and updates on the reader
before beta approval. See [the add-on guide](../ADDONS.md).
