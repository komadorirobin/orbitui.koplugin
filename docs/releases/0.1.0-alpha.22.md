# OrbitUI 0.1.0-alpha.22

Photographic author ornaments, plus Svevo and Hemingway.
**Preview release: physical Bigme appearance, touch and migration acceptance
remain outstanding.**

## Changes

- Replace Strindberg, Hamsun, Dostoevsky, Dylan Thomas, Lem, Musil and Mann
  with cutouts of photographs of real sculptures. Original material colours
  and details are preserved through alpha masking and proportional resizing;
  no AI reconstruction or invented anatomy. Hamsun's source is monochrome.
- Mann uses Molgreen's Wikimedia photograph of Gustav Seitz's bronze portrait.
  The previously prepared AI-assisted Seitz image is not included in this
  package or the published commit ancestry.
- Add Italo Svevo and Ernest Hemingway in the independent **Authors II** pack,
  using photographs of the Trieste and Cojimar sculptures. They appear beside
  the matching authors' books, with offline Swedish biographies and tap-to-zoom.
- Retire the generated Clarice Lispector default. Only its exact known bytes
  are removed; custom replacements are retained. No Rio replacement or Dazai
  ornament is bundled in this release.
- Existing artwork upgrades are checksum-guarded and resumable. Custom images,
  captions, manual positioning, disabled pieces, native overrides, linked paths
  and deleted files are preserved. Joyce, Woolf and Kafka are unchanged.

Photo credits, sources, modifications and separate sculpture-rights notes are
included with each pack. Individual images retain their own license conditions;
this is not a worldwide sculpture-rights waiver or a relicensing of the whole
collection. The existing Woolf adaptation retains its non-commercial terms.

No upstream merge: Bookshelf remains at 5.3.1 through `74b825bb` and SimpleUI
at 2.7.5 through `b58dfefb`. Reading data, synchronization settings and navigation
are unchanged.

## Updating

Open **Tools > OrbitUI > Uppdatera OrbitUI > Check for updates**, with
**Include preview releases** enabled. Install alpha.22 and restart KOReader.
Alpha.21 and earlier alpha releases can update directly with bootstrap/API 1
unchanged. Allow roughly 250 MiB free in addition to installed versions.

Use **orbitui.koplugin.zip**, not GitHub's source-code archive. Ornaments,
credits and migrations are included; no separate ornament download is needed.
Do not manually overwrite customized ornament folders to obtain the updates.

[OTA and recovery](https://github.com/komadorirobin/orbitui.koplugin/blob/v0.1.0-alpha.22/docs/OTA.md)
describes code rollback. It retains copied artwork and metadata, and does not
restore removed or replaced images. Keep backups for asset-level rollback.

## Verification

Release gates cover full Lua/LuaJIT runs: 372 OrbitUI cases, 82 Python checks,
351 Bookshelf suites, all 13 SimpleUI files and 41 translation catalogs.
Two native-SQLite suites and existing font-dependent cases remain explicit
skips. Original-photo and museum-pixel audits run locally with source caches;
CI skips the four cache-dependent checks. Clean packaging, commit ancestry,
native OTA from the actual alpha.21 installer, downloaded draft assets, both CI
jobs and anonymous public OTA are checked before completing publication.

These are headless checks, not physical-device acceptance. On the Bigme, check
the new silhouettes and base alignment, matching to authors, zoom and biography
scrolling. Hemingway's enlarged image is limited by the original photo detail.

See [ornament sources and licenses](https://github.com/komadorirobin/orbitui.koplugin/blob/v0.1.0-alpha.22/docs/ORNAMENTS.md).
