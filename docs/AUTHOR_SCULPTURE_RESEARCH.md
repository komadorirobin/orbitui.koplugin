# Real sculpture replacement candidates

Researched 2026-10-06 at the user's request. The user has now approved proceeding
with all six candidates below, choosing the Geneva Musil photograph instead
of the restricted Belvedere image. All six are now masked and integrated locally. Joyce and
Woolf were explicitly excluded. Kafka stays unchanged. Lispector's generated
image is already retired; its separate Rio preview remains outside the bundle.

Preserve a sculpture's actual silhouette, material and viewpoint. Do not invent
shoulders, plinths or plaster finishes. A source photograph's license does not
automatically clear the underlying sculpture or every type of adaptation.
The statuses below are research evidence, not worldwide legal clearance.

## Candidates

- **Ernest Hemingway:** Fernando Boada's bronze in Cojimar, Cuba, 1962.
  Use Carol M. Highsmith's 6144x4096 original, LC-DIG-highsm-06293 /
  [LCCN2010638901](https://www.loc.gov/item/2010638901/), not 06294/2010638902:
  the latter shows the bust much farther away. A recorded source crop retains
  the detail before masking; the head is not reconstructed or sharpened by AI.
  [Highsmith photo rights](https://www.loc.gov/rr/print/res/482_high.html) explicitly

  Credit the sculptor; do not claim a worldwide waiver of sculpture rights.
  Destination-country rights remain a public-redistribution review item.

Sources, masks, crop and hashes are recorded in `scripts/artwork/sculpture-additions.json`

## Mann: Molgreen photographic replacement (2026-10-06, superseded)

The user approved [Molgreen's close view from 11 April 2024](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:20240411_xl_0704-Thomas_Mann_B%C3%BCste_3.jpg),
3072 x 4080, CC BY-SA 4.0. It has better usable detail than the wider Molgreen

without AI redrawing, recolouring or invented anatomy. Alpha masking removes
the background and separate granite pedestal; head, neck, mounting block and
thin bronze plinth remain. Reviewed on both light and dark backgrounds.

The license covers the photograph/adaptation, not ownership of the sculpture.
The documented separate basis is [German UrhG section 59](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__59.html)
for the permanently installed, publicly visible Berlin work. [Section 62](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__62.html)
addresses alterations (including resizing), and [section 63](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__63.html)
requires attribution. This is the project review basis for a photographic
depiction, not a worldwide rights waiver or a claim of Foundation permission.
The old Ahrens/image_gen adaptation remains held and is not bundled at HEAD;
its unpublished ancestor commits must also stay off the public remote.

`mann-photo-cutout.json` + the reviewed mask reproduce the image. The existing
`mann-seitz.json` provenance path now records Molgreen. A v2 migration preserves
custom artwork and recognizes either prior stock bitmap plus partial retries.
No public release or rewrite of Git history is part of the image replacement.

## Earlier six-author implementation checkpoint

All six selected source photographs stay outside the repo in
`~/OrbitUI-artwork/sculpture-research/`. Only the transparent cutouts, reviewed
alpha masks and provenance are committed. No private-use Belvedere image is
bundled. Nothing has been published; the old Mann adaptation's hold remains.

The first image_gen extraction of Lem was rejected because it changed the
bronze texture. Following the user's approval, use original photograph pixels,
hand-traced masks with edge refinement, then proportional Lanczos resizing.
No generation, colour changes or reconstructed sculpture details. The masks
remove vegetation, museum surroundings and separate display pedestals, while
retaining the real sculptural base where visible. Dylan's mount ends at the
source photo's lower edge; Dostoevsky's enlarged preview is limited by the
smaller sculpture area in the source. No detail is invented to hide that.

`scripts/build-sculpture-cutouts.py` builds all six 1024 x 1536 RGBA PNGs from
the approved masks. `scripts/artwork/author-sculptures.json` records source,
mask and output hashes, licenses, actual sculptors and placement. Light/dark
previews were visually reviewed. Biographies and native tap-to-zoom remain.

`core/orbitui_sculpture_updates.lua` is connected before generic biography
updates. Its independent marker and frozen baseline preserve custom artwork,
captions, placements, links and deletions; interrupted batches resume. Joyce,
Woolf, Kafka and the already integrated Mann image bytes remain untouched.
See TESTING.md for automated checks and the outstanding physical-device check.
