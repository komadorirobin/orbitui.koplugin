Manga miniatures for OrbitUI

Three colour ornaments with transparent backgrounds and tap-to-zoom info:
- Kaneda Motorcycle: Akira.
- Walking Mushroom: Dungeon Meshi / Delicious in Dungeon.
- Apothecary Mortar: The Apothecary Diaries / Kusuriya no Hitorigoto.

The first two are unofficial AI-generated fan-art interpretations, not photos
of licensed figures. The mortar is an original generic apothecary still life.
See ATTRIBUTION.txt and prompts.json for the individual rights/provenance notes.
Do not treat the fan art as CC0, official artwork or blanket-cleared character IP.

OrbitUI installs this pack once into settings/bookshelf/ornaments/Manga.
Enable it in the shelf's theme/ornaments browser if your theme uses selected
packs. Existing pack switches, per-piece switches and the None frequency are
respected. Later user edits and deletions are never automatically reversed.

Matching uses exact series metadata aliases, not title/filename substrings.
The ornaments stay beside their own series boxes or volumes, never in the
random deck or on empty shelves. At most one appears per physical shelf row.
Author busts keep priority and their multiple-bust exception. A matching run
may receive a new miniature at the start of the next page. If two different
series share a row, only the first eligible one gets an ornament.

The original PNG bytes and alpha are preserved. The pack's placement defaults
allow for transparent margins at the native ornament size; the reader can
adjust size, height and spacing in the normal ornament editor.
