Manga III - quiet nights and ordinary days

Two original AI-generated thematic miniatures for OrbitUI:
- Stargazing Telescope: Insomniacs After School.
- Quiet Bungalow: Hirayasumi.

These are generic original designs inspired by the series' themes, not exact
replicas of manga props, buildings, character figures or commercial products.
Full prompts, unmodified PNG hashes and contextual sources are in prompts.json.
See ATTRIBUTION.txt for artwork credit and rights notes.

The pack is installed additively under settings/bookshelf/ornaments/Manga III.
Its independent ornament-manga-iii-v1.installed marker preserves previously
installed packs, customized files and later deletions. No theme is activated
and no existing ornament settings are changed.

Enable Manga III in your theme's ornament collection if it selects specific
packs. None, pack and individual off switches remain authoritative. These
pieces appear only beside matching series metadata, including series boxes
and individual volumes. English, romanized Japanese and Japanese names are
supported. Titles, filenames and arbitrary folder names do not trigger them.
They never enter the random ornament deck or decorate bare shelves.

At most one ordinary ornament appears per physical row. Author busts retain
priority and their multiple-bust exception. A series continuing on the next
page may receive its ornament again. Tap for the native enlarged view and
English information card; long-press for placement/size controls.

The original colour and alpha channels are retained. The small negative lift
only compensates for transparent pixels below the object, placing its visible
foot on the shelf. It is not a wall-hung ornament. No image generation, model
rendering or network access takes place on the reader.

Disable Manga III before rolling back to alpha.51 or earlier: those versions
do not recognize its series bindings and could treat the pieces as random.
Desktop previews and automated tests are not physical e-ink device acceptance.
