AUTHORS v1 - AUTHOR-MATCHED SHELF ORNAMENTS
Eight additional busts for OrbitUI:
August Strindberg, Stanislaw Lem, Dylan Thomas, Thomas Mann, Fyodor Dostoevsky,
Knut Hamsun, Robert Musil and Franz Kafka.

Installed once from the bundled runtime into:
koreader/settings/bookshelf/ornaments/Authors/

This separate pack adds to Modernists without changing the Joyce/Woolf images,
user adjustments, disabled pieces, or previously deleted files/packs.
Each pack has its own one-time installation marker. Interrupted copies retry
on next startup; existing files are not overwritten. Deleting this pack after
installation does not cause a later startup to restore it.

Enabled busts appear before the first book in the matching author's run,
including page starts. They never enter the ordinary random ornament deck.
Matching uses full author metadata, including inverted surnames, not titles or
filenames. Lem's accented and ASCII spellings both work. Swedish Dostojevskij
and common English Dostoevsky/Dostoyevsky forms are also supported.
No match is guessed from a multi-book folder's representative.

Find the pack in Wallpaper, ornaments and colours > Ornament collection > Authors.
Tap a bust to zoom and read about the author's life, writing and major works.
These Swedish, AI-assisted OrbitUI summaries include source links and retain
the separate artwork credits below the biography. All text is available offline.
Long-press for scale, padding and height.
Shelf frequency None, pack disable and per-piece disable remain effective.
A narrow row shrinks or omits the bust rather than separating it from its book.

Eight 1024 x 1536 RGBA PNGs. Seven are masked photographs of real sculptures;
only Kafka retains its earlier AI-assisted photographic extraction.
The photographic cutouts preserve photographed surfaces, material colours and
anatomy; no generated details or invented plinths. Hamsun stays monochrome.
Small scale/lift adjustments align the visible bases; no per-page network calls
or image generation are involved. prompts.json contains per-image provenance.
ATTRIBUTION.txt records the separate image licenses and modifications.
Dostoevsky now uses an independent CC BY-SA 3.0 photo, not the earlier NC scan
adaptation. Lem/Kafka are CC BY-SA 4.0; other photo terms are listed individually.
Photograph licenses do not license underlying sculpture rights. Separate
bases and limits for each depicted work are documented in the notices.

A one-time caption update replaces only unchanged published info fields and
this default README. Custom captions, native reader overrides, placements,
disabled pieces, deleted entries and files are preserved. Image bytes change
only in separately marked, checksum-guarded artwork migrations. Text lives in
scripts/artwork/author-biographies.json in the OrbitUI source repository.

AUTHOR-SCULPTURES.txt documents the six new photographs and their exact hashes.
Strindberg (Eldh), Hamsun (Frolich), Dostoevsky (Laveretsky), Dylan Thomas
(de Wet), Lem (Kurkowski) and Musil (Bavaud, Geneva) replace only recognized old
default images. Recognized old captions, default placement triples and shared
notices migrate too. Custom images, edited captions, deleted files and links
are left alone. Interrupted updates resume safely on the next startup.

Kafka now uses an AI-assisted cutout of the bronze Kielce bust, with its hat
and bronze colour retained. KAFKA-KIELCE.txt identifies the photographer,
sculptor, license, modifications and the separate sculpture rights basis.
A separately marked update replaces only the checksum-recognized old default
Kafka PNG. It updates recognized captions/placement and unedited notices,
without resetting native overrides, custom images or deleted pieces/packs.

Mann now uses Molgreen's 2024 Wikimedia photograph of Gustav Seitz's bronze
portrait, masked without AI redrawing. THOMAS-MANN-SEITZ.txt records the
CC BY-SA 4.0 photo/cutout license and separate German panorama basis. The
superseded AI-assisted Mann image remains excluded from public distribution.
A separate v2 checksum-guarded migration recognizes both the original generated
default and the local AI-assisted cutout, plus known captions, placement triples
and notices. The older v1 completion marker does not block this replacement.
Custom artwork, captions, settings, linked files and deleted pieces remain
untouched. The original author's biography, matching and tap-to-zoom remain.

The former generated Clarice Lispector portrait has been retired at the user's
request. A one-time update removes only its exact published PNG checksum from
Authors, never a custom replacement or a linked file/folder. Existing captions,
placement overrides, notices and all other artwork are retained. Author matching
for a custom Lispector image still works. No replacement Lispector image is
bundled; the separate Rio preview is not cleared for public distribution.

Older OrbitUI versions that do not know this pack may place it randomly.
Disable Authors before rolling back to alpha.14 or earlier if that is unwanted.
