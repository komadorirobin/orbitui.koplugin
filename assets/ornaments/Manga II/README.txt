Manga miniatures II for OrbitUI

Eleven colour ornaments with transparent backgrounds, tap-to-zoom and context:
- Nyanko Sensei: Natsume's Book of Friends.
- Brushbuddy: Witch Hat Atelier.
- Ohmu: Nausicaa of the Valley of the Wind.
- Mimic Chest: Frieren: Beyond Journey's End.
- Pochita: Chainsaw Man.
- Turbo Granny: Dandadan.
- Transponder Snail: One Piece.
- Kettenkrad: Girls' Last Tour.
- Camp Tent: Laid-Back Camp.
- Armillary Sphere: Orb: On the Movements of the Earth.
- Cafe Alpha: Yokohama Kaidashi Kikou.

The first eight are unofficial AI-generated fan-art interpretations. The last
three are original generic, thematic still lifes, not replicas of manga props.
Context captions are original summaries with source links, not copied product
descriptions. See ATTRIBUTION.txt and prompts.json for provenance/rights notes.

OrbitUI installs this independent pack once into settings/bookshelf/ornaments/
Manga II. Enable Manga II in the shelf's theme/ornaments browser if your theme
uses selected packs. No existing pack selection is changed automatically.
The original Manga pack is untouched, including edited/deleted files.
Later changes or deletions in Manga II are also preserved after installation.

Matching uses exact series metadata aliases, including the known Omnibus and
Deluxe Edition names. Straight/curly apostrophes and Nausicaa/Nausicaa with an
umlaut are equivalent. Titles, filenames and author names never imply a match.
Each piece stays beside its series box or volumes. It never appears randomly
or on an empty row. There is at most one ordinary ornament per physical row;
author busts keep priority and their multiple-bust exception. Disabled shelves,
packs and individual pieces remain disabled.

Original generated PNG bytes and alpha are preserved. Native scale/lift/pad
defaults account for the transparent margins so visible feet meet the plank.
You can still change these values in the normal ornament editor.
