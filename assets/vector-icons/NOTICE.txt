Manga (OrbitUI), outline and duotone variants
An OrbitUI adaptation, not an official Solar or Tabler icon. Combines the book
silhouette and page lines from Solar Book 2 with the hiragana strokes from
Tabler language-hiragana. The Latin letter is removed, the Japanese mark is
rescaled/repositioned on the cover, strokes are matched to Solar, and the
duotone version uses a half-opacity book with a solid mark. No font is needed.
The adaptation is distributed under CC BY 4.0, with the Tabler MIT notice also
retained. Please credit Solar / 480 Design, Tabler / Pawel Kuna and OrbitUI.
No endorsement by the original authors is implied.
